Period-comparison visualization — iterations

Five ways to read one story: revenue grew, but the segment that matters is declining. Current period uses a blue monochrome ramp — the highlighted segment is always the darkest stop. The reference period is fully gray. Every iteration carries the same reference switcher.

Iteration 1 · Linked share-shift barsOptimizes for: one-glance reading in a sales call — the flow bands make the share shift physical, the key segment visibly narrows without reading a number. Trade-off: bands encode share, not euros; absolute growth lives only in the headline and edge totals. Weakens past ~5 segments. Hover a segment for value, share shift and the period path.

Iteration 2 · Segment ledgerOptimizes for: answering "did each part perform better or worse" in euros — the health question is about value, not share. Trend chips are always visible, no hover needed. Scales cleanly to 6–7 segments. Trade-off: the part-to-whole reading disappears; requires row-by-row scanning rather than one gestalt. Hover a row for the period path.

Current total · highlighted segmentOther segmentsReference total

Iteration 3 · Totals with segment deltasOptimizes for: the simplest possible expansion — two aggregated totals, current always on top, and on click each segment appears as its plain difference vs the reference on the same axis. Direction is position: left of zero declined, right grew. No cumulative arithmetic to explain. Trade-off: deltas are small next to the totals, so the axis under-uses its range; shares aren't shown.

Iteration 4 · Share slope panelOptimizes for: direction — every segment's movement is a literal slope, and the crossing point (sponsored overtaking organic) is impossible to miss. The most compact of the five. Trade-off: only two data points per segment; magnitudes are positional, not sized, so it's weakest at communicating euros. Hover a line for values; left axis is the reference, right is current.

Iteration 5 · Stacked pair with period pathOptimizes for: the collapsed/expanded interaction in the brief — light view is two bars, current vs reference; expanding inserts every intermediate period between them so the path is persistent on screen, not trapped in a tooltip. Survives projectors and screen shares. Trade-off: expanded state is tall; intermediate bars repeat the same encoding, so it rewards scanning more than glancing.
